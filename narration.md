# Tadcu — Episode 4: "The Wall"
## Narration (voice-over) script — recording copy

*One narrator, same voice. Episodes 1–3 told the sheep story out of
order — this one's about a man, a wall, and the honesty of cement.
Music bed: soft harp + a workmanlike snare.

Character lines stay as on-screen subtitle bubbles, shown in *italics*
under each beat for the editor only. **BOLD = narrator, to be recorded.**

---

### ⏱ 0:00 — TITLE CARD

**(NARRATOR, cold open)**
> **A man who has spent his life opening gates has decided that the whole
> problem was the gate all along. The wall, then. The wall.**

---

### ⏱ 0:05.5 — WORKING DAY (scene 2)

**0:06.1**
> **Summer found Tadcu with a plan, a wheelbarrow, and a wall's
> worth of ambition.**

*(Tadcu addresses the girls. Bryn holds the string.)*

**0:14.7**
> **The girls nodded. Sheep nod like that.**

---

### ⏱ 0:19.5 — THE WALL (scene 3)

**0:20.3**
> **Cement on. Stone on. Trowel off. A rhythm you could dance to
> — everybody but the cement did.**

*(The practices: paw prints in the course. Tadcu discovers them.)*

**0:29.1**
> **Decorative, said Bryn. Load-bearing, said Dilys's county
> cousin.**

---

### ⏱ 0:34.5 — THE RAIN (scene 4)

**0:35.3**
> **The sky, for comic effect, opened at half two.**

*(Tadcu: "the buckets, quick!" The girls watch. The word 'drizzling'
gets a lot of use.)*

**0:43.1**
> **Buckets brought. Drips caught. Words said that do not appear
> in tutorials.**

*(Tadcu's verdict on the now-cured wall.)*

**0:46.7**
> **It had turned one shade of grey back into history.**

---

### ⏱ 0:48.5 — THE GATE (scene 5)

**0:49.3**
> **The wall stood firm. The wall, in fact, was a beauty.**

*(Tadcu pats it. Tadcu looks for the gate. Tadcu's face does the sums.)*

**0:53.1**
> **The gate was inside the wall.**

*(Beat. Bryn brings the chisel.)*

**0:57.9**
> **There are two kinds of men: those who build the gate into the
> wall, and those who build the wall around the gate.**

*(Beat.)*

**1:00.3**
> **Tadcu, that afternoon, was the first kind.**

---

### ⏱ 1:03.5 — HOME (scene 6)

**1:04.3**
> **At dusk, chisel lessons concluded, the gate came out — one inch
> higher, very slightly tilted, and entirely its own.**

*(Tadcu, milk-stout: "Better. Latched. Every day.")*

**1:08.5**
> **And the girls filed in, past the paw-printed wall, into the field
> that never had a wall before.**

*(Tadcu to camera.)*

**1:12.1**
> **And the wall did its job. It kept the story out — for exactly
> a week.**

---

### ⏱ 1:16.5 — END CARD (scene 7)

**1:18.7**
> **Nos da, Cwm Twp. Build strong, build level — but leave the gate
> a window.**

*MUSIC OUT.*

---

## Recording notes

- **Runtime:** ~80 seconds; ~10 narration lines, ≈200 words.
- **The montage (0:19–0:34)**: workmanlike tempo, keep the strum feel.
- **"The gate was inside the wall" (0:53)**: the biggest laugh of the
  series so far. Dead flat. One comma of air. Then Bryn's chisel.
- **"The first kind" (1:00)**: the closest this show gets to a punchline
  sweater — deliver the pause then the matter-of-fact verdict.
