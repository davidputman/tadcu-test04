Ep. 4 · production run “test04” · Cwm Twp production unit
Narrator Summer found Tadcu with a plan, a wheelbarrow, and a wall’s worth of ambition.
Tadcu One wall, girls. This wall here. It goes up, you stay in, everybody’s happy.
Narrator The girls nodded. Sheep nod like that.
Bryn (holds the string. supervises.)
Tadcu Cement on, stone on, trowel OFF — and mind the gate, that’s the vital part.
Narrator The girls applauded, from a distance of one suspicious field.
Tadcu Bryn — PAW PRINTS. All over the fresh course!
Narrator Decorative, said Bryn. Load-bearing, said Dilys’s county cousin.
Narrator The sky, for comic effect, opened at half two.
Tadcu Rain? NOW? The wall won’t SET — the buckets, quick!
Narrator Buckets brought, drips caught, words said that do not appear in tutorials.
Tadcu It’s a shade of grey now. A bit... soggy-artistic.
Narrator The wall stood firm. The wall, in fact, was a beauty.
Tadcu So good the girls can’t get OUT. ...Bryn. WHERE’S the gate?
Narrator INSIDE the wall. The gate was inside the wall.
Tadcu I can’t get IN, Bryn. I’m locked out of my own field.
Bryn (brings the chisel. professional.)
Narrator At dusk, chisel lessons concluded, the gate came out — one inch higher, very slightly tilted, and entirely its own.
Tadcu Better. Latched. Every day, mind.
Narrator And the girls filed in, past the paw-printed wall — into the field that never had a wall before.
Tadcu Nos da, chi gyd. The wall did its job: it kept the story out.
