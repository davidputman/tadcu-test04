# Tadcu — Episode 4: "The Wall"

An episode of the **Tadcu** animated cartoon series. Tadcu (Welsh for
"Grandpa"), his sheepdog Bryn, ninety-nine ewes with opinions about
building sites, and a wall that gets a little too good at its job.

## How to watch

Open `index.html` in a browser, press ▶ (or space). Controls: play/pause,
restart, scene skip, click the bar to scrub by scene, keys 1–7 to jump to a
scene.

## Episode 4 summary

Tadcu has had enough: one wall, built properly, so the girls stay in the
field for good. Bryn supervises and signs the fresh cement wherever the
trowel isn't looking. The sky opens at the exact wrong moment (half past
two), the buckets come out, and the finished wall is, the job reports, a
beauty — so good that the gate ends up INSIDE it. Nobody gets in. Bryn
fetches the chisel, dusk brings chisel lessons, and the field ends
latched, level, and one inch higher than before.

## New in this episode

- **The wall** — staggered courses, load-bearing paw prints, a lopsided
  sense of purpose.
- **The cement** — grey, patient, arguably rain-soluble at half two.
- **The chisel** — whichever of the two of them, no names, swung the hammer.
- **The gate** — still the canniest character in the show.
