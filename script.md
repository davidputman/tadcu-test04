### SCENE 1 — WORKING DAY (0:05.5–0:19.5)

**EXT. THE FIELD'S EAST WALL — MORNING**

Cement bags, wheelbarrow, one wall already half-built — and the flock

4. **TADCU:** "Bryn — paw prints. ALL OVER the fresh course."
- **NARRATOR:** "Decorative, said Bryn. Load-bearing, said Dilys's county

### SCENE 3 — THE RAIN (0:34.5–0:48.5)

**EXT. SAME — 2:30, DRAMATICALLY**

- **NARRATOR:** "The sky, for comic effect, opened at half two."
- **TADCU:** "Rain? NOW? The wall won't set — the buckets, quick!"
- **NARRATOR:** "Buckets brought, drips caught, words said that don't
  appear in tutorials."
- **TADCU:** "It's a shade of grey now. A bit... soggy-artistic."

### SCENE 4 — THE GATE (0:48.5–1:03.5)

**EXT. THE FINISHED WALL — TRIUMPHANT**

- **NARRATOR:** "The wall stood firm. The wall, in fact, was a beauty."
- **TADCU:** "So good the girls can't get OUT. ...Bryn. WHERE'S the gate?"
- **NARRATOR:** "INSIDE the wall. The gate was inside the wall."
- **TADCU:** "I can't get IN, Bryn. I'm locked out of my own field."
- **BRYN:** *(brings the chisel. professional.)*

### SCENE 5 — HOME (1:03.5–1:16.5)

**EXT. THE COTTAGE — DUSK**

Chisel lessons concluded; the gate re-seats itself, one inch higher,
very slightly tilted, entirely self-aware.

- **NARRATOR:** "At dusk, the gate came out — one inch higher, very
  slightly tilted, all its own."
- **TADCU:** "Better. Latched. Every day, mind."
- **NARRATOR:** "And the girls filed in past the paw-printed wall, into
  the field that never had a wall before."
- **TADCU:** "Nos da, chi gyd. The wall did its job: it kept the story out."
